PRE-LAUNCH POLICY

Privacy

ARCUS Campaign Kit is currently in pre-launch testing and does not accept public paid transactions.

The production application is designed so a user-authorized Pollinations token remains in browser session storage and is not intentionally written to application logs, analytics, URLs, or persistent browser storage.

Business brief content submitted to the application may be sent to Pollinations for generation after the user explicitly authorizes Pollinations access. Pollinations' own privacy terms apply to that processing.

Before public launch, this notice will be updated to describe any account, billing, analytics, support, and retention systems that are enabled in production.
