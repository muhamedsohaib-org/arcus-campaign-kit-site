BYOP ARCHITECTURE

Customer-funded inference.

ARCUS Campaign Kit uses Pollinations Connect User Wallets / BYOP. Customers authorize generation against their own Pollinations balance rather than ARCUS silently subsidizing model usage.

The commercial product fee pays for the workflow, interface and campaign-generation system. AI usage remains transparent and separately authorized by the customer.

FAQ

What buyers need to know.

What does ARCUS Campaign Kit sell?

A software workflow that turns a marketing brief into a structured campaign pack.

Does ARCUS include unlimited AI generation?

No. Pollinations usage is authorized separately through BYOP and remains tied to the customer's Pollinations balance.

Is the product available now?

The product is currently in pre-launch testing. Public paid checkout will only be enabled after payment onboarding and production QA are complete.

Who operates this product?

ARCUS is the product organization responsible for Campaign Kit. Source and infrastructure are maintained under the ARCUS GitHub organization.
